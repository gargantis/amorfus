# v-look

I attacked the look team's claims and the game constraints, with all paths relative to S = .. I ran the two engine-copy candidates (the look team's mbo-look.mjs, imported unchanged) at n 20 with the exact PLAN caps 0.15/0.05, at n 20 with the vertical cap at 0 as the look team defined them, and at n 24. Each ran over 11 catalogue scenes plus 18 new ones: a 2-high room, two trenches, and paint scenes on 2-thick ground (lines, an L-turn, crossings, a checker, pixel letters, a stripe joined to a patch, a line across a step). I also measured seams on labels against the render field D in five regimes at n 16/24, checked the closed forms, re-ran the 2D seam at n 256 and probed the listed regimes for L1. Claim (1) splits. The constraint half holds: tunnel, room, trench, single, pillar, flat ground (exactly flat only with vertical cap 0) and visible rounding all pass at n 20/24. The paint half is refuted. Any paint that does not span the full slab thickness pools, merges or melts. A diagonal line on 2-thick ground drains into the slab corners, or becomes one round blob when it lies 2+ blocks from the edges. A checker becomes one region, the letters A and Z become two ovals, and a stripe drains into a joined patch. With the PLAN vertical cap at n 24, even the 1-thick band breaks, and paint fidelity 0.05 or 0.10 does not fix any of this. Claim (2) is partly true: under the candidate the seam stays exactly flat on the labels. Under FID-0.15 the seam column stays put but the faces pillow up one sub-cell, so the rendered groove is blur plus a terrace. Local's closed form is the λ = 0, g = 1 special case; a λ- and g-aware version matches the rendered field to ≤1e-4. Real label grooves also form under FINITE-4 at n 24, and the Young angle is approached only in 2D (119.4° at n 256). L1 fails at n 20, where NOVOL-1 and CAGE-0.05 keep the tunnel at exactly 1.90 × 0.70; NOVOL-1 still fails the room. Local L9 fails in a long L shape, where an edge's rounding changes by one sub-cell.

## new findings
- With nearly free paint (paint fidelity 0.02, plus volume kept), painted patterns on ground two or more blocks thick behave like free bubbles inside the solid. Each painted cluster minimises its boundary with the base material. It moves to where it touches the most air (slab corners and edges, a step's convex edge), sinks through the slab there, or contracts into a lens. This is the earlier smoothing study's 'flood and melt' failure, in a stronger form, and it happens at the look team's own n 16. The 1-thick catalogue slabs hid it.
- No paint fidelity tested gives both a straight band and stable paint. At 0.02 and 0.05 the band is straight (SD 0.05 / 0.09) but paint floods. At 0.10 flooding is weaker but still present (big2-line area x1.25, one blob; text 4/63 blocks wrong) and the band zigzags back (SD 0.29). Inference: per-cell paint fidelity cannot separate 'straighten a staircase' from 'move paint by blocks'.
- Under PLAN's vertical cap (one sub-cell at n 24), the 1-thick painted diagonal drains into the slab corners for both k (middle band 0.01-0.03, B reconnecting across the junctions). At n 20 with the same cap it holds. The band result is therefore resolution-dependent even on 1-thick slabs.
- Paint coupling reaches across the whole connected paint cluster. A 1-wide stripe joined to a 3x3 patch drains completely into the patch (stripe width 0 along its length; top paint area x0.54; 7.0 block^3 sunk). A lone stripe instead pools at the slab edge it touches (end width 3.0). On flat ground the candidates' paint scenes did not converge in 150 steps.
- The render field D is not a neutral picture of the labels. It raises a 1-thick slab's flat top by 0.02-0.08 (render pressure 0.13-0.19), its seam groove depends on g and on that pressure, and small clusters render much rounder than their labels: CAGE-0.05's single block is a near-cube on labels (diagonal/axis 1.42) but a lumpy ball in the image. Judging 'visibly rounded' from images overstates rounding (the last point is inference from images).
- Under FID-0.15 the 2-high room closes completely (clear height 0 at the back, 1.125 x 0.375 mid-room at n 16), worse than the tunnel. Under the candidate it keeps 2.0 x 1.70, but its walls bow inward to the 0.15 cap and the trench narrows to exactly 0.70 at its floor. Concave corners fill up to the cap everywhere.
## open
- Can paint relax only within the top surface (a 2D problem on the material map), with per-block area conservation instead of per-cluster volume? That might keep the straightened diagonal without pooling, sinking or flooding. Untested.
- Does a pixel-art-aware rule (smooth only recognised digital lines, leave small shapes blocky) avoid the melt this bubble model shows? The earlier smoothing study names it as the untested middle path.
- The seam case where a seam meets a convex corner (local L12) was not tested.
- 3D Neumann angles at n <= 24 could not be measured reliably (circle fits on a small window disagreed: 89-106 deg), so the 3D angle question is left to the 2D runs.
- Is the box candidate's constraint pass worth anything if it holds only by construction (caps), when every other part of its behaviour is set by the global per-cluster pressure (non-local and slow, 150 steps not converged on paint scenes)?
